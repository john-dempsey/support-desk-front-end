The Login and Register Pages

Two forms written inside their pages: what they send, how they leave once someone is logged in, and how they show the API's errors.

  • The Login Page

    LoginPage holds what's typed, calls login() from useAuth(), and on success does nothing at all: the new user makes the page leave by itself.

  • Leaving Once Logged In

    If there's a user, LoginPage renders a <Navigate> to the ticket list. One rule covers someone who has just logged in and someone who was already logged in.

  • When Login Fails

    A wrong password shows the API's message at the top of the form. An email the browser accepts but the API doesn't shows its message next to the email field, with Stage 3's FieldError.

  • describeAuthError

    Two of the API's auth errors need saying in the user's own words: too many attempts, and an email that's already registered. A small function in utils/ rewords them for both pages.

  • The Register Page

    RegisterPage is LoginPage with a name field, a password rule, and register() instead of login(). A new account is always a customer, and is logged in straight away.
