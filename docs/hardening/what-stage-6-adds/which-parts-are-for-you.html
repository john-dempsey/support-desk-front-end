Which Parts Are for You

Most of Stage 6 needs nothing from your API, so it works on a Stage 3 app with no login. Only the nginx topic needs Docker.

Stages 4 and 5 each needed a step of the back-end assignment. Most of Stage 6 needs nothing from your API at all. It works on whatever app you have, even one that stopped at Stage 3 because its API has no login.

If your app stopped at Stage 3

Do topics 02 to 07 on your Stage 3 app. Everything they change is already there: pages that load data, a route table, a ticket list (or your own list), and tests.

A few things in the Support Desk code only exist because of Stages 4 and 5: the login and register pages, ProtectedRoute and the notifications page. When a slide changes one of those, skip that part. Nothing else depends on them.

If your app is your own case study

As in every stage, the Support Desk code shows the pattern, and your app applies it to its own pages. Your pages load different things and have different names, but they follow the same pattern that topic 02 pulls out into a hook. So the hook itself carries over almost word for word.

Try it

Make a list of your own app's pages that load data from the API. For each one, write down the src/api/ function it calls. Keep the list: topic 03 moves each of those pages onto the new hook.
