Not Colour Alone

The badge's colour helps people scan the list, but its words carry the meaning, and every colour pair passes WCAG AA.

The starter's accessibility topic set a rule: never show information only with colour. StatusBadge follows it.

The words carry the meaning

The colour helps you scan a long list: the blue badges stand out from the grey ones. But every badge also says its status in words. Someone who can't tell yellow from green, or who uses a screen reader, still gets the whole message, because the screen reader reads "In progress", and the colour adds nothing they need.

The badge's outline helps too. border: 1px solid currentColor draws the border in the same colour as the text, so the badge's shape stays visible even when its background colour is very close to the page's.

Contrast

Every text colour passes the WCAG AA ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its own background:

Badge Text Background Ratio
Open #0550ae #ddf4ff 6.7:1
In progress #7d4e00 #fff8c5 6.6:1
Resolved #116329 #dafbe1 6.6:1
Closed (and unknown) #59636e #f6f8fa 5.7:1

These colours are written straight into the module rather than as variables in index.css, because nothing outside the badge uses them.

Try it

In Chrome's developer tools, open the Rendering panel (the ⋮ menu, then More tools, then Rendering). Find Emulate vision deficiencies and choose Achromatopsia, which shows the page with no colour at all. Can you still tell every ticket's status? How? Set it back to No emulation when you're done.
